Your timeline. Your call.

miuzika is an experimental music workstation in your browser, built on openDAW. Generate parts with open music models, play them live with your touchpad and keyboard, and arrange everything in a timeline you control. There is no chatbot in between: what you type goes to the model as it is, and the same request does the same thing every time.

What it does

A studio in a browser tab, where the models are instruments you play, not assistants you talk to.

A real DAW

Timeline, piano roll, the 128 General MIDI instruments and 12 drum kits, on openDAW's audio engine compiled to WebAssembly. Export to MP3, WAV or MIDI.

Play it live

Your touchpad and your keyboard become instruments. Record in a loop, layer over layer, with note repeat, erase and undo for each pass.

Generate with open models

Songs, single instruments and sound effects from text with ACE-Step and Stable Audio. Then cover, repaint a few bars or continue what you have.

Split and transcribe

Separate a song into stems with Demucs, and turn an audio track into MIDI with Basic Pitch, on your own computer.

Generative patterns

Write a Strudel pattern, hear it, and add it to the project as an audio track at the bar you choose.

Licences in plain sight

Every GPU module states its model's licence before you run it, and every generated take records which model, commit, parameters and seed made it.

How it works

Each model has its own form with every control it really has. Nothing rewrites your request on the way.

You fill the form

Text, lyrics, tempo, key, the bars to change, the tracks to use as a source. It is sent exactly as written.

You review it

The full request, the model's licence and what the GPU will bill, before anything runs.

It runs on a GPU

A cloud GPU on your own account starts, runs the model and stops. You see each step, and Off is one click away.

It lands in the timeline

As a new track at the bar you chose, ready to play, cut and arrange. Repeat runs it again with the same seed.

Models and their licences

They are not bundled: each module installs its model at a pinned version when it runs.

ACE-Stepsongs and parts from text; cover, repaint, extract, complete · MIT
Stable Audiosongs, instruments, sound effects; transform, repaint, continue · Stability AI Community License (free under US$1M yearly revenue)
MuLaCovera cover of a song with your lyrics and style · CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial only
Demucsstem separation · MIT

Strudelgenerative patterns, in your browser · AGPL-3.0

A take made from a known song keeps that song's rights, whatever the model's licence.

Getting started

miuzika runs on your own computer. You need Node 24, Python 3 with numpy and ffmpeg.

The generative modules need a GPU account

They run on a Lambda cloud GPU with your own account and SSH key, which bills by the hour while it is on. Stable Audio also needs a Hugging Face token that has accepted its terms. Without them you still have the whole workstation, the Pad, Strudel and To MIDI.

Install

Clone the repository and install its dependencies.

git clone https://github.com/ccarrillomanzanares/miuzika.git
cd miuzika
npm install

Add the instruments

Download the GeneralUser GS SoundFont and put its .sf2 in workspace/catalogo/gm/.

Start it

Then open http://localhost:5180.

npm run dev

Make music

Click New and name the project. Open the Pad and play, or pick a model in Modules. GPU accounts go in Settings.

Projects are saved to workspace/proyectos/ as you work. More detail in the README.

Where it stands

miuzika runs locally, on your own computer, and changes quickly. There is no online version or user accounts yet.

Working now

  • Projects with autosave; drop in WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG and MIDI
  • Instrument picker: General MIDI, drum kits, openDAW synths
  • The Pad: touchpad and keyboard, loop recording
  • ACE-Step, Stable Audio, MuLaCover, Demucs and Strudel modules
  • Audio to MIDI; export to MP3, WAV and MIDI

Not yet

  • A hosted version: it starts with npm run dev
  • Volume, pan and effects controls in the interface
  • Touchpad capture outside Windows
  • Long audio stems following the Pad's loop
